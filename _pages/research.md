## Translational science across disciplines

My work spans **molecular biology, immunology, infectious disease, computational biology, and clinical translation**. I have deliberately worked across disciplinary boundaries, using different experimental and analytical approaches to address biological questions that are difficult to resolve within a single field.

My work spans distinct biological problems, but the underlying approach is consistent: **define the question, develop measurements that can discriminate among competing explanations, quantify the biology, and translate the result into something useful.**

---

## Areas of Research

### 01 — Noncanonical DNA & Extracellular Biology

#### Can the physical structure of extracellular DNA influence biological function?

My current work investigates whether the **physical organization and molecular conformation of extracellular DNA** contribute to the biology of neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs), with particular interest in Z-DNA and other noncanonical DNA structures.

I have contributed to this work through **experimental design, quantitative image analysis, data interpretation, and scientific writing**, working closely with experimental collaborators who perform the underlying bench studies.

My analytical work has focused on developing quantitative approaches to characterize DNA architecture, spatial organization, structural remodeling, and nuclease resistance within NETs, and using these measurements to build and test mechanistic models of extracellular DNA function.

This work brings together **experimental biology, quantitative imaging, computational analysis, and biophysical reasoning** to investigate whether DNA structure itself can contribute to extracellular immune and antimicrobial function.

### 02 — Antimicrobial Resistance & Rapid Phenotypic Diagnostics

#### Can phenotypic antimicrobial susceptibility testing become practical for routine gonorrhea care?

Unlike many bacterial infections, **phenotypic antimicrobial susceptibility testing is not routinely performed for uncomplicated gonorrhea in clinical practice**. Treatment therefore frequently relies on empiric use of ceftriaxone rather than susceptibility-guided therapy.

The challenge is not simply making existing AST faster. **New technologies and workflows may be required to make phenotypic AST sufficiently rapid, practical, scalable, and clinically actionable for routine use in gonorrhea.**

My work in *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* explores rapid phenotypic approaches that could ultimately enable susceptibility-guided treatment, helping preserve ceftriaxone effectiveness while providing clinicians with biologically meaningful information about antimicrobial response.

This work sits at the intersection of:

**Microbiology · Antimicrobial Resistance · Phenotypic AST · Diagnostic Technology · Clinical Translation**

### 03 — Translational & Clinical Biology

#### How can molecular measurements become clinically meaningful information?

A substantial part of my work has focused on connecting molecular biology with human disease and clinically relevant phenotypes.

Areas of work and interest include:

- **Biomarker discovery and validation**
- **Host-response biology**
- **Infectious disease and inflammation**
- **Sepsis**
- **Clinical biobanks**
- **Molecular diagnostics**
- **Multi-omics integration**
- **Patient-derived biological data**

The goal is not simply to identify molecular differences, but to determine **which measurements provide reproducible, biologically meaningful, and clinically useful information**.

# How I Approach Biological Problems

Different biological questions require different technologies. My approach, however, is consistent.

### 1. Define the biological question

What are we actually trying to explain?

### 2. Separate observation from interpretation

What does the evidence demonstrate—and what are we assuming?

### 3. Identify competing mechanisms

What alternative explanations could produce the same observation?

### 4. Design the discriminating experiment

Which experiment most effectively distinguishes between those mechanisms?

### 5. Quantify the biology

Where possible, convert qualitative observations into measurable phenotypes.

### 6. Connect mechanism to function

Why does the biological phenomenon matter?

### 7. Translate

Can the insight inform diagnostics, therapeutics, clinical decision-making, or future research?
