My publication record spans **microbial genomics, computational biology, infectious disease, molecular diagnostics, quantitative phenotyping, and extracellular DNA biology**.

Across these areas, a consistent theme has been the development and application of quantitative approaches to understand complex biological systems and translate molecular or cellular measurements into biologically and clinically meaningful information.

## Extracellular DNA, NETs & Noncanonical Nucleic Acids

My recent work has increasingly focused on the biology of extracellular nucleic acids and the possibility that their **molecular structure and biochemical properties contribute directly to immune and antimicrobial function**.

### Co-first-author research

**Z-DNA is an Intrinsic Structural Component of Neutrophil Extracellular Traps**  
*TD Demir, N Ram-Mohan, S Yang*  
*bioRxiv*, 2026

**Co-first author**

This primary research study investigates the presence, organization, and persistence of Z-DNA within neutrophil extracellular traps and develops a quantitative framework for examining its structural relationship with extracellular DNA architecture.

### Related research

**Neutrophil extracellular traps have active DNAzymes that promote bactericidal activity**  
*TH Ku, N Ram-Mohan, EJ Zudock, R Abe, S Yang*  
*Nucleic Acids Research*, 2024

This work established a functional perspective on extracellular DNA within NETs, demonstrating that NET-associated DNA can possess catalytic activity relevant to bactericidal function.

### Perspective & synthesis

**Unravelling the noncanonical extracellular DNA structures in biofilm and NETosis**  
*TD Demir, S Azechi-Ogawa, N Ram-Mohan, S Yang*  
*Nucleic Acids Research*, 2026

This perspective examines emerging evidence for noncanonical extracellular DNA structures across biofilms and NETosis and places current work in the broader context of extracellular nucleic-acid biology.
